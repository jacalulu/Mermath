# Mermath — Brand Guidelines

*v1, November 2026. Drafted after the design-direction sessions on the map screen,
with a Gemini creative-director pass over the live screens. These guidelines sit
above `visual-language.md` (the token-level rules) and below nothing: when a
screen and this document disagree, the screen is wrong.*

## 1. Brand essence

**Tactile. Joyful. Gentle.**

Mermath is a safe, hand-painted underwater world where early math feels like
discovering treasure — never like a test.

## 2. The two-layer rule

Everything on screen is a painting.

- **Layer 1 — the world.** Scenery, characters, props: brush-stroked, diegetic,
  alive. Nothing in this layer is tappable chrome.
- **Layer 2 — what floats.** Names, counters, buttons: they may float *above*
  the painting, but they must be **made of paint** — painted lettering, painted
  shadows, soft zone-tinted washes. If a toddler touched it, it should feel
  like thick paint softened by water.

The failures that taught us this rule (all tried, all rejected): vector pills,
driftwood signs planted underwater, glassmorphism materials, flat app-style
buttons, stark contour outlines. UI may not pretend to be scenery, and it may
not look like it was pasted in from an operating system.

## 3. Color

Paint with light and water, under strict value discipline: every region of a
screen is deliberately dark or deliberately bright, so that ink can sit on it
without outlines.

**Anchors (locked):**
- **Pearl cream `#FFF6E0`** — primary ink on dark grounds; highlights.
- **Midnight navy `#0B2545`** — primary ink on light grounds; deep shadow.
- **Tap-me orange `#FF9A3D`** — means "tap me" / "you got it" and *nothing*
  else. Never decoration, never environment.
- **Sister colors (pink / lemon / purple)** — only ever on Ophelia, Daphne and
  Violet. Never in environments or UI, so the girls always own the eye.

**Zone sub-palettes:**
- *Tide Pool* — sandy ochres, warm shallow teals, kelp greens.
- *Coral Reef* — vibrant turquoise, sunlit cyan, coral/magenta accents.
- *Deep Sea* — deep indigos, midnight navy, bioluminescent cyan glints.
- *Grotto (bedroom)* — cozy indigo dusk, string-light gold, soft shadows.

## 4. Typography

**Roles:**
- **Display — painted lockups.** Place names, screen titles, hero moments.
  Generated by `art-pipeline/lettering.py` from the chunky hand-drawn skeleton
  (Chewy today; *Mermath Display*, our own cut, is the roadmap) and painted by
  the brush pipeline. The words are objects of the world, not type.
- **Text & numerals — Baloo 2.** Speech bubbles, numerals on fish and shells,
  counters, parent screen. Locked.

**Hard rules for display lettering:**
1. **No outlines, ever.** Separation comes from ink value and one soft painted
   shadow (offset toward lower-right, matching the world's light).
2. **Ink follows ground.** Cream on dark, navy on light — chosen per placement.
3. **Whisper bounce.** ±1.5° rotation, ±2px baseline per letter. Playful, not
   janky; the bounce is a rhythm, never a scatter.
4. **Pocket washes.** A name on variable ground carries a soft brushed
   zone-tinted wash behind it (the painted scrim), strong enough to pass the
   legibility floor, soft enough to read as a pool of light or shade.
5. **Brush texture always.** Lockups ship through the painter; raw vector
   lettering never reaches the screen.

**The pearl gag:** one pearl nests in the counter of the first *o* of a
display lockup. One per lockup, display only — never in Baloo text, never in
numerals, never forced where there is no o.

## 5. Shape & material language

Sharp corners do not exist underwater.

- **Buttons and controls** look like water-worn painted pebbles and shells:
  chunky, forgiving silhouettes with painted gradients and soft painted
  shadows. Big enough for a three-year-old's thumb (≥ 64pt touch target), and
  made through the art recipe like any other prop.
- **Speech bubbles and panels** are thick creamy paint with organic, swooping
  tails — never vector-perfect geometry.
- **The tap-me language**: orange plus a gentle swell is the only "press me"
  signal; controls breathe, they do not pulse frantically.

## 6. Motion

1. **Water-suspended.** Everything drifts, bobs or floats in; nothing snaps or
   pops into existence.
2. **The game waits for the child.** No urgency cues, no idle-shaming; prompts
   repeat kindly on request.
3. **Success is organic celebration** — a shimmy, painted bubbles, a chime —
   never flashes or shakes.
4. **Touch feels watery.** Taps answer with a soft squash-and-stretch bloop.
5. All motion honors Reduce Motion (the world holds still, feedback is
   instant).

## 7. Legibility (measured, not eyeballed)

Display text holds **≥ 3:1** (WCAG large-text) against the *worst* part of the
ground it touches, targeting 4.5:1 — judged on the shipped frame by
`tools/check-contrast.py`, which segments the painted letters and measures the
ring around them (per Xbox Accessibility Guideline 102). When the meter flags
a spot: first move the label, then strengthen its pocket. New screens and new
lockups run the check before they land.

## 8. Never list

- Never flat vector shapes, stock icons or generated images in the world.
- Never contour outlines or crisp digital drop shadows on lettering.
- Never tap-me orange on anything that is not a control or a success.
- Never sister colors off the sisters.
- Never perfect pills, sharp rectangles, or OS-flavored chrome.
- Never timers, lives, countdowns or failure states.
- Never text-only instruction — every prompt is spoken.
- Never ship a label below the contrast floor.

## 9. Gaps to close (audit of current screens, Nov 2026)

1. **Pearl counter** — currently a vector pill with geometric circles; redesign
   as painted shells/pearls on a strand.
2. **Speech bubbles** — vector-perfect tails and edges; repaint with organic
   swoops through the art recipe.
3. **Buttons** ("Open the chest", "Dive again", map/home icons) — digital pill
   and circle shapes; rebuild as painted pebble/shell controls (orange usage is
   correct; the shapes are not).
4. **Grotto empty slots** — dashed vector circles; repaint as soft brushed
   bubble rings.
5. **Map composition** — the frame itself needs the key-art pass (value
   structure, foreground framing, light as subject) so Layer 2 has deliberate
   quiet zones to live in.
